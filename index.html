Making it

  1. Toast the oat flour.
  2. Weigh everything into a large bowl. Whisk two full minutes — longer than feels necessary. Uneven mixing is the main way a batch goes wrong.
  3. Transfer to an airtight opaque container. Cool cupboard, not the fridge; condensation is a bigger enemy than warmth.
  4. Stir the container before each scoop. Fine powders settle, and cocoa sinks fastest.
  5. Per shake: liquid into the blender first, then the powder. Room temperature, not cold — whey resists cold liquid and clumps. Blend 30–45 seconds, add ice after.
  6. Rest 15–20 minutes in the fridge before drinking. It thickens considerably and is easier on your stomach.

Multivitamin taken separately, a couple of hours apart — the calcium and phytates here suppress iron absorption.
